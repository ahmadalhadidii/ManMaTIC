MANMATIC
Man . Machine . Technology
Ahmad Al-Hadidii · Prospective Human–Machine Institute · Aqaba, Jordan
MANMATIC is a site-specific institution in Aqaba, Jordan, organized around one primary aim: to prepare humans to understand, train with, and work alongside intelligent machines before those systems enter real operation. Its program is derived first from Aqaba’s operational reality—logistics, industry, and infrastructure—while climate pressure and future economic transformation shape its broader role. Through architecture, the project turns this relationship into a clear spatial system of preparation, oversight, and gradual integration.
