MANMATIC
Man . Machine . Technology
Ahmad Al-Hadidii · Prospective Human–Machine Institute · Aqaba, Jordan
MANMATIC is a site-specific institution in Aqaba, Jordan, organized around one primary aim: to prepare humans to understand, train with, and work alongside intelligent machines before those systems enter real operation. Its program is derived first from Aqaba’s operational reality—logistics, industry, and infrastructure—while climate pressure and future economic transformation shape its broader role. Through architecture, the project turns this relationship into a clear spatial system of preparation, oversight, and gradual integration.

DESIGN PROCESS

Ahmad Al-Hadidii — Project Author and Design Owner. Orion — Design-Thinking Partner.

The project was developed through iterative discussion, critique, testing, and refinement. Ahmad remained the primary decision-maker and project owner across concept, plan logic, and spatial judgment. Orion contributed as a design partner in structuring arguments, research synthesis, wording, and comparative testing, which strengthened community engagement framing and made the project broader, clearer, and easier to understand for different audiences. The process drew on 112 reviewed boards, 74 case-study references, and 120 research / technical sources.

Every line in the plan, and every decision within it, was the result of dialogue and critical discussion; these relationships are represented in this graph.
